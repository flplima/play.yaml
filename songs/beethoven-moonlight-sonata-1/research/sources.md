# Sources: Moonlight Sonata, first movement

- **MuseTrainer public-domain library**:
  <https://musetrainer.github.io/library/scores/Sonate_No._14_Moonlight_1st_Movement.mxl>
  MusicXML engraving (MuseScore 2.2.1, 2018), 69 bars, 4/4, four sharps. The
  source of every note. Only my conversion is committed.
- **1802 first edition (Cappi, Vienna), plate 879**:
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sonata_quasi_una_fantasia.pdf>
  Scan of the original print, public domain (published 1802). Used to read the
