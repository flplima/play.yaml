# Sources: Charleston

- **The 1923 Harms edition, scanned (Johns Hopkins, Levy Sheet Music Collection)**:
  <https://archive.org/details/carner_6_297>.
  "The Original Charleston", words and music by Cecil Mack and Jimmy Johnson,
  New York: Harms Inc., "Copyright MCMXXIII", plate 6870-4. Six pages: cover,
  four pages of music (voice, piano, ukulele chord boxes by Charles N. Grant),
  and a back page of dance steps by Oscar Duryea. Licence: public domain. The
  song was published in the US in 1923, and a plain scan of an old print adds
  no new copyright. The Internet Archive lists it in the Johns Hopkins
  Sheridan Libraries' collection of African American contributors. Used for
  **every note, word and mark**, from the 500 ppi page images inside the PDF.
  The file was read in a scratch folder and is not committed.
  Trust: high. Clean print, every notehead readable.

- **The same edition at the University of Alberta**:
  <https://archive.org/details/SM002492>.
  "Original Charleston", Harms, 1923, plate 6870-4, with the same ukulele
  arranger. The library marks it "deemed public domain". Its catalogue notes
  give the first lines of the verse and the refrain and say the back cover has
  the dance directions, which matches the Johns Hopkins copy. Used only for
  that metadata.

- **IMSLP, Charleston (Various)**:
  <https://imslp.org/wiki/Charleston_(Various)>.
  Lists a scan of the same edition (T.B. Harms, 1923, plate 6870-4, file
  #880807, public domain) and a 2026 engraving by Zoe Newstead (PDF #1058462
  and MuseScore #1058463, CC BY-SA 4.0). IMSLP's downloads sit behind a bot
  check, so neither file was used.

- **University of South Florida, Black American Sheet Music Collection**:
  <https://digitalcommons.usf.edu/aa_sheet_music/6>.
  The same 1923 Harms publication, six pages, rights statement "No Copyright,
  United States". It gives the lifespans used in the README: Johnson 1894 to
  1955, Mack 1873 to 1944. The PDF download is behind a Cloudflare check, so
  the scan was not used.

- **A reference MIDI for `play-yaml-fidelity`**: none found with a clear free
  licence, so the score has no fidelity number. The check is the two readings
  and the engraved comparison described in `../README.md`.
