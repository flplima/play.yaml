# Sources — BWV 881

## Fugue

- **Kyle Rother, LilyPond edition** — IMSLP file **#497295**
  (`fugue12_expanded.ly.zip`). The header states it was "prepared and typeset
  … using the 1866 Breitkopf & Härtel Bach-Gesellschaft Ausgabe as primary
  source", with reference to "both the Henle and Bärenreiter urtext editions,
  as well as the critical and scholarly commentary of Alfred Dürr", and that
  "This edition is in the public domain, and the editor does not claim any
  rights in the content."
  Trust: high — a scholarly edition that names its sources. Rendered to MIDI
  with LilyPond 2.26.0 and imported note for note.

## Prelude

- **Yo Tomita, MIDI sequence of WTC Book II** — originally
  `music.qub.ac.uk/~tomita/midi/Wtcii12a.mid`, linked from Dave's J.S. Bach
  Page (<http://www.jsbach.net/midi/midi_wtc2.html>). The host now returns
  HTTP 500; recovered from the Internet Archive capture of 2000-08-24:
  <https://web.archive.org/web/20000824144635if_/http://www.music.qub.ac.uk/~tomita/midi/Wtcii12a.mid>
  Trust: high for pitch and rhythm — Tomita is a Bach scholar and the sequence
  is essentially gridded (0.0011 beat mean deviation); the expressive tempo map
  is his and is not used, since the importer works in beats.

Bach is public domain; both editions above are freely licensed. Per the repo's
copyright rule neither file is committed — only the transcription, with the
attribution here and in the score header.
