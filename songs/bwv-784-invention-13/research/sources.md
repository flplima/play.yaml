# Sources — BWV 784

- **Mutopia Project, LilyPond edition** —
  <https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/BachJS/BWV784/bach-invention-13/>
  Source `.ly` and rendered `.mid`. The typesetter placed the engraving in the
  public domain outright ("This sheet music has been placed in the public
  domain by the typesetter"), and Bach has been public domain since 1750. The
  `.ly` uses `\include "deutsch.ly"` — German note names — which is where the
  previous score's unreadable `gis4` tokens came from.
  Trust: high. Used for **all the notes**; the rendered MIDI is mechanical.
  Only the transcription is committed, per the repo's copyright rule.

- **IMSLP #864228**, `invention-no13-A-min.pdf` — a LilyPond engraving of the
  same piece, used only to eyeball that the barring and the ending (a fermata
  over the final chord) match. Public domain.
  Trust: medium as an independent check — it is also LilyPond-typeset, so it
  may share ancestry with the Mutopia file.
