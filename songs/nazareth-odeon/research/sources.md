# Sources: Odeon

- **Ernesto Nazareth 150 anos (Instituto Moreira Salles), work page**:
  <https://ernestonazareth150anos.com.br/Works/view/136>.
  History in brief: composed and first published in 1909 by Casa Mozart
  (E. Bevilacqua & Cia.), paid for by Nazareth, dedicated to Zambelli & Cia., who
  owned the Cinema Odeon where he played. The notes add that the autograph and
  his 1913 recording (with Pedro de Alcântara on piccolo) repeat B at the end,
  for the form ABACABA.
  Trust: high for the history.

- **The first edition, scanned**:
  <https://ernestonazareth150anos.com.br/files/uploads/work_elements/work_136/odeon.pdf>.
  Rio de Janeiro: E. Bevilacqua & Cia., ca. 1909, plate 6602, 3 pages, "Tango
  para piano". Licence: public domain. The music has been free since Nazareth's
  death in 1934 plus 70 years, and a plain scan of a 1909 print adds no new
  copyright. Used for **every note, mark and the form**. The file was read in a
  scratch folder and is not committed.
  Trust: high. Clean enough to read every notehead, with a few print blemishes.

- **IMSLP, Odeon (Nazareth, Ernesto)**:
  <https://imslp.org/wiki/Odeon_(Nazareth,_Ernesto)>.
  Lists the same Bevilacqua edition as #600163 (public domain), plus the
  autograph scanned by the Biblioteca Nacional (#613830), a 1926 edition
  (#04456) and the Musica Brasilis engraving (#613834, CC BY-SA 4.0). IMSLP's
  downloads sit behind a bot check, so the scan came from the IMS site instead.
  No MIDI or MusicXML is listed.

- **Musica Brasilis edition**:
  <https://musicabrasilis.org.br/partituras/ernesto-nazareth-odeon>,
  PDF <https://musicabrasilis.com/media/en_odeon.pdf>.
  Engraved by Luciana Requião and Mônica Leme, revised by Alexandre Dias against
  the autograph at the Biblioteca Nacional. Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0 for the
  engraving and its editorial work. Used only as a second reading of each bar:
  its music font gives exact notehead positions. It agrees with 1909 on every
  note. None of its own marks (its form with a coda, its trio accents, its
  hairpin) were taken. Not committed.
  Trust: high as a cross-check.

- **The autograph, Biblioteca Nacional**:
  <http://objdigital.bn.br/objdigital2/acervo_digital/div_musica/mas484561/mas484561.pdf>.
  The download timed out, so it was not consulted.

- **A reference MIDI for `play-yaml-fidelity`**: none found with a clear free
  licence, so the score has no fidelity number. The check is the two-print
